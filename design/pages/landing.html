How it works

As easy as a note on the fridge

1.

Write your list

Paste a link from any shop and we'll fill in the name, picture and price. Or just jot it down — "something cosy to read" counts.

2.

Share one link

Send it by text, email or the family group chat. Friends open it on any phone or laptop.

3.

They claim, you're surprised

Friends tick off what they're buying so nobody doubles up. You never see what's been claimed.

No spoilers

Same list, two views

You see your list exactly as you wrote it. Your friends see what's still up for grabs. Claimed and bought gifts stay hidden from you until the big day.
